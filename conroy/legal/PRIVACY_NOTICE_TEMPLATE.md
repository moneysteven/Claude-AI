# Privacy notice template: [BUSINESS NAME]'s AI assistant

> **IMPORTANT: have a Jamaican attorney review this before you publish it.** This is a starting template written by a non-lawyer for businesses using the Conroy AI receptionist. It reflects the Jamaica Data Protection Act, 2020 (JDPA) as understood in November 2026. It is not legal advice. Section references, the list of rights and the wording of the lawful bases must be checked against the Act, the Data Protection Regulations 2024 and current guidance from the Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC).
>
> How to use it: replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`, delete options that don't apply, and publish it on the business's website (footer link). Then set the business's "Privacy notice URL" in Conroy (dashboard → Settings → Business & assistant) so the AI notices in chat and WhatsApp link to it. Until then, Conroy links to a shorter generated notice at `/p/[slug]/privacy`.

## 2. You are talking to an AI assistant

We use an AI assistant called **[ASSISTANT NAME]** to:
- answer phone calls, WhatsApp messages and our website chat;
- book, change and cancel appointments;
- take messages, and send confirmations, reminders and payment links.

[ASSISTANT NAME] is a computer program, not a person, and always says so at the start of a call or chat.

- **Phone calls may be recorded and transcribed** so we can serve you, check what was agreed and improve our service. We tell you this at the start of every call.
- **You can ask for a person at any time.** On the phone say "I want to talk to a person". In a chat type "talk to a person". We will connect you or a team member will reply.
- The assistant does not give medical, legal or financial advice. [For clinics: it only books appointments and passes messages to our staff. In an emergency, call 110 (ambulance/fire) or 119 (police).]

## 3. What personal data we collect

We collect only what we need to help you:

| Data | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Contact details | your name, phone/WhatsApp number | you, and caller ID / your WhatsApp profile |
| Your request | the service, date and time you want, how urgent it is, notes you give us | you |
| Booking records | appointments, booking references, which staff member, attendance | created when you book |
| Conversations | call recordings, call transcripts and summaries, WhatsApp and website chat messages | your calls and chats |
| Payment records | payment requests, amounts, references, whether paid | us; payment providers |
| Technical data | for the website chat: a random chat token stored in your browser, your device's time zone (to show appointment times), your IP address (security and abuse prevention) | your browser |

We **never** ask for or store your card number. Card payments are made on the payment provider's secure page ([Fygaro / WiPay]), not with us.

Please **do not share** medical details, ID numbers, passwords or financial information with the assistant.

## 8. Your rights

[Attorney to confirm the exact rights and how they are exercised under the JDPA.] You may:
- **ask for a copy** of the personal data we hold about you (access);
- **ask us to correct** inaccurate data;
- **ask us to stop** or limit processing that is likely to cause you damage or distress;
- **tell us to stop** using your data for direct marketing (including WhatsApp: reply STOP);

- **withdraw consent** where we rely on it.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 1. We may ask you to confirm your identity (for example, by messaging from the phone number we hold). We will reply within [30 days / the period required by law].

## 9. Security

The system we use protects your data with:
- encryption in transit (HTTPS) and encryption at rest of call transcripts, recordings links, messages and account credentials;
- access limited to our staff accounts, with an audit log;
- separation of our data from other businesses using the same system;
- regular backups.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data in a way that the law requires us to report, we will notify the Information Commissioner and you as required.

## 10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the **Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC)**, Jamaica: [address and contact details from https://oic.gov.jm].
