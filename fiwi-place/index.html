About FiWi Place

A Garden Setting for Life’s Finest Moments

Tucked into a quiet garden setting, FiWi Place brings together scratch-made catering, bespoke menus and warm hospitality for the celebrations that matter most: baby showers, birthdays, wedding brunch and private dinners.

Every event is built around you: your guest list, your menu, your vision. Our team handles the details so you can enjoy the moment.
