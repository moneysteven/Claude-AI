About FiWi Place

A Garden Setting for Life’s Finest Moments

Tucked into a quiet garden setting, FiWi Place brings an authentic experience to our customers: real food, real local flavors, and warm hospitality for the celebrations that matter most — baby showers, birthdays, weddings, private dinners, and full-service catering.
