03Smile Recall

Smile Recall.

A quiet system that remembers your next visit, so your smile is not left to chance.

  • Cleaning and check-up remindersA note when about six months have passed since your last visit.
  • A follow-up after treatmentA short check-in a week or so after a filling, extraction or root canal.
  • A kind word if it has been a whileIf a year has gone by, an invitation to come back for a check-up.

No sales messages and no spam. Reminders are sent personally by the team on WhatsApp or by phone. A person presses Send each time; nothing goes out automatically. You can ask to be removed at any time.

OverdueDue soon6-month cleaning / check-up recall: due when last visit + 182 days is on or before today. “Due soon” in the 21 days before that.
Post-treatment follow-upIf a record has a treatment note (for example extraction, root canal, fillings), a follow-up is due 10 days after the last visit. Shown from day 7 to day 21.
Win-back (12+ months)Last visit 365 or more days ago. Replaces the cleaning recall message for that patient.
Birthday this monthBirth month and day fall in the current calendar month, or within the next 7 days.
