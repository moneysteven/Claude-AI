Good to know

Questions, answered

Can you help with a venue for my wedding or event?

Yes — we can connect you with Fiwi Place for the venue, chair and table rental, and catering. Just tick the Fiwi Place options on the booking form.

How do I book?

Fill in the booking form, or message us on WhatsApp at (876) 568-5668. Tell us the service, date and time, and we’ll confirm your booking.

How do parents find their school photos?

Every photo has an IMG number. Go to Client Proofing, enter the access code from your school, then search your child’s IMG # to view and order.

I already have an ID card design. Can you print it?

Absolutely. Choose “I have a design and want to print” when booking, then send your file on WhatsApp or by email.

Where is the studio?

Shop #15 Hendon Mall, Beckford Street, Savanna-la-Mar, Westmoreland. Get directions on Google Maps.
