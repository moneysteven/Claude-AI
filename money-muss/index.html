Over 11 years in Sav-la-mar

Money Muss sits in Lewisville Plaza, at the end of KFC Road. For over 11 years, people in Savanna-la-mar have come here for authentic Jamaican breakfast and lunch, a drink from a full bar and a turn on the games.

It is also where the community celebrates. Each year we raise a cup at Cups Up, our anniversary party, and put on the Back To School Treat & After Party with prizes and giveaways.
