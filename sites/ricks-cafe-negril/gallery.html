Rick’s Cafeturns 47!

“Rick’s Cafe turns 47!” · official Ricks Cafe Online channel · 47 seconds · released for the 47th anniversary (about 2021).

What you’ll see: the animated Rick’s logo, drone views over the cliffs and cove, guests watching the sunset in silhouette, a professional pole dive and the sun setting over the sea. Plays from YouTube, with sound, only when you press play.

This is historical footage. The dive is part of the Rick’s story; it isn’t a guide to jumping. Read the cliff safety notes.
