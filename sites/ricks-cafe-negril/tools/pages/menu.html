Prices
“All prices are in U.S. dollars, are subject to change & do not include GCT or 10% service charge.” Substitutions are allowed, “depending on the mood of the Chef.”
{{ico:alert}}
Gluten & allergies
As printed: “*Salads are gluten free. Entrees are gluten free when ordered with rice and peas. We are not a gluten-free kitchen, but we do our best to ensure that cross-contamination does not occur.” If you have an allergy, talk to your server before ordering.
{{ico:waves}}
Lobster season
“We support Jamaica’s environmental conservation efforts and serve lobster only from July 1st – March 31st.” Availability on the day isn’t guaranteed.
