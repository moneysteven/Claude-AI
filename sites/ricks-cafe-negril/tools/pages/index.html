Rick's Cafe

Welcome to Rick's Cafe

A Jamaicantradition.

Richard “Rick” Hershman opened Rick’s on Negril’s West End cliffs in April 1974, when Negril was still a sleepy fishing village. Two hurricanes and two rebuilds later, people still come for the same things: the view, the food, the rum punch and the cliffs.
