FAQ

Goodquestions.

Answers from the official Rick’s FAQ and menu. The FAQ is undated, so for anything important, call or email before you go.

Is there an admission charge?

No. Admission is free, and the homepage says “No Cover Charge.”

What are the opening hours?

Rick’s publishes noon to 10 PM, every day of the year. A third-party guide lists 9:30 PM, and holiday hours aren’t published, so please confirm before you travel. All times are Jamaica time (UTC−5, no daylight saving).

Can I bring children?

Yes, children are allowed (official FAQ). It’s a busy, lively bar atmosphere, especially around sunset.

What should I wear?

The FAQ recommends tropical light clothing, with flat or gym shoes.

Can I bring my pet?

The FAQ’s answer: “Due to the crowds, it would not be safe.”

Is there a pool?

Yes. Pool hours, depth and supervision aren’t published, so please ask the team on site.

Can I jump off the cliffs?

The FAQ says: “Yes, At your own risk.” It lists jump levels of 10 ft (3 m), 25 ft (7.6 m) and 35 ft (10.6 m), and water 15 ft (4.5 m) deep. Those are published figures, not a guarantee of today’s tides, conditions or clearance. Check conditions and follow staff guidance on site. Read the full safety notes.

Do you take dinner reservations?

“Small groups (under 10) – NO. Larger groups – YES.” Call 876-957-0380 or come in. Exactly 10 people? Please ask when you call.

Can I reserve a cabana?

Yes, in advance. The FAQ lists Cabana 1 at $250 USD a day and Cabanas 2, 3 and 4 at $200 USD a day each (tax included, about 10 people). Those rates are undated, so confirm them when you book. Arrange cabanas by phone or email; this website never asks for card details. More on cabanas.

Which cards do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Discover and American Express (official FAQ). Other payment methods aren’t listed, so ask on site.

Are tax and service included in menu prices?

No. The menu says prices “are in U.S. dollars, are subject to change & do not include GCT or 10% service charge.” The FAQ says GCT is 15% and a typical gratuity is 10–15%. Check your bill before adding a tip.

Is there a gift shop?

Yes: one-of-a-kind shirts, hats, towels, bags, mugs and more. There are no online sales yet (official FAQ).

How do I get there from my hotel?

The FAQ suggests asking your hotel’s tour desk. Map and directions.

Can Rick’s host my wedding or group?

Yes. Rick’s hosts weddings, corporate retreats, reunions, anniversaries and more. Email info@rickscafejamaica.com. Groups, weddings & cabanas.

Is Rick’s a hotel?

No. Rick’s is a cliffside bar and restaurant on the West End: no overnight rooms, and it isn’t on Seven Mile Beach.

Is Rick’s open after Hurricane Melissa?

AFAR reported on 23 January 2026 that Rick’s in Negril had reopened by mid-December 2025. For today’s status, call or email before you go.

See you on the West End.

Questions before you go? Email info@rickscafejamaica.com or call either published number.
