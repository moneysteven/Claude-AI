Timeline

Fifty-plus yearson the West End.

  1. 1973

    Tom Martin arrives in Negril

    The 2026 menu names Tom Martin as President of Rick’s Cafe. It says he moved to Negril in 1973 and partnered with Hershman soon after, and that he’s “full of stories of the early days and happy to share them.”

  2. April 1974

    Rick finds the cliffs

    Richard “Rick” Hershman discovers the cliffside spot. The official site describes Negril then as a sleepy fishing village without electricity, telephone or hot running water. The land belonged to a local doctor, son of independent Jamaica’s first Governor General; the Jamaica Observer (2015) names him as Dr Winston Campbell, son of Sir Clifford Campbell, and says Hershman bought the one-and-a-half-acre property that year.

  3. The 1970s

    A sunset tradition begins

    Rick’s describes itself as the first public bar and restaurant of its type on the West End cliffs, an alternative to Seven Mile Beach, and it quickly became a gathering spot for the sunset.

  4. Sept 1988

    Hurricane Gilbert

    Gilbert destroys Rick’s completely. The business recalls waves of over 25 feet. Rick’s is rebuilt, “stronger than ever.”

  5. 11 Sept 2004

    Hurricane Ivan

    Sixteen years later, Ivan, which the menu calls “the most destructive storm to date,” brings waves the business puts at 80 feet and reduces Rick’s “to kindling wood.” It is rebuilt again, “bigger and better than ever.”

  6. 2021

    Rick’s turns 47

    The official channel releases the anniversary film “Rick’s Cafe turns 47!” Watch it in the gallery.

  7. 2024

    Fifty years

    Rick’s marks its 50 Year Anniversary Celebration, featured on the official homepage.

  8. Oct 2025

    Hurricane Melissa

    On 23 January 2026, AFAR reported that Rick’s in Negril had reopened by mid-December, after Hurricane Melissa struck in October 2025.

  9. 2026

    Year 52

    The 2026 menu (Revision 26.03) celebrates the 52nd anniversary: “Thanks for making it Rick’s. Walk Good Mon…”

The Rick's family

The peoplebehind the bar.

The official homepage counts over 100 staff, and the 2026 menu lists a team of 115 to serve guests and look after the 1.5-acre property. Many have been part of the family for more than 30 years.

Staff figures are as published at the time and change over the years.
