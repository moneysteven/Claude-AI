Welcome to Chappa Auto

The home of
affordable auto parts.

Since 2003, Chappa Auto has helped Jamaican drivers and mechanics keep their vehicles running safely. What began as one shop in Westmoreland now has branches in Kingston, St. James, Hanover and Westmoreland.

Walk in for brake parts, tyres and Shell lubricants, or ask about wholesale supply for your garage, fleet or service station.
