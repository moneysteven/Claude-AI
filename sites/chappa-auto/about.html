Our story

One shop.Four parishes.

Ms. Hazel Tomlinson started Chappa Auto in 2003 at Tanka Plaza, 60 Beckford Street, Savanna-la-Mar. She wanted drivers and mechanics in Savanna-la-Mar and the surrounding areas to have access to reliable, high-quality automotive products.

Over two decades the business has grown into a multi-branch operation selling and distributing auto parts, lubricants and accessories, with branches in Westmoreland, St. James, Hanover and Kingston.

To confirm The About page says six locations; the Contact page lists five. It also spells the first shop's plaza “Tanka Plaza”, while the Contact page prints “Tanker Plaza” and “Savanna La Mar”.
