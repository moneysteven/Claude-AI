# Little Cafe — demo website

A single-file, no-backend demo site for Little Cafe, Station Road, Little London,
Westmoreland, Jamaica. Open `index.html` in any browser, or upload it to any static host.

Everything (styles, scripts, the vector logo, 17 photos and 8 short clips) is embedded
in that one file, so it works offline and needs no build step.

## What's on the page

- Intro film: the 15-second dish montage plays full-screen when the site opens, with each dish's
  name titled over its shot, then a crisp logo card ("Station Road, Little London · Free delivery
  12pm–5pm") that dissolves into the page. It plays once per visit, can be skipped (button or Esc),
  and "Watch the intro film" in the hero replays it.
- Light or dark: the site follows the device's appearance setting. The orange delivery band, the
  night reel wall, the paper review tickets and the mahogany footer keep one look in both.
- Hero: "Little" writes itself on, and a fanned stack of dish clips is dealt and reshuffled every
  few seconds, in step with the headline ("Hearty plates of ackee & saltfish..."). A spinning
  "Free delivery · 12pm to 5pm" stamp sits on top. A pause button and dish dots control it.
- Crossed ribbons of dish names, a scroll-lit About statement with tiny food photos set into the text,
  and an orange delivery band whose clock face lights up 12 to 5.
- Menu: on desktop, scroll or hover a dish and the large plate beside it switches to that dish's clip
  and colour; on phones each dish card plays its clip when it's in view.
- Gallery: a dark reel wall of all 8 clips (hover or tap to play, full-screen viewer with captions).
- Reviews shown as kitchen order tickets on a rail (placeholders only), a pulsing approximate-area map,
  a giant tap-to-call number, and a scrolling "Quality taste guaranteed" footer.
- Everything respects "reduce motion": no autoplay, no moving bands, all text fully shown.

## Notes

- Photos are stills taken from the owner's own Instagram reels (720p), not the
  low-resolution Instagram thumbnails, so nothing shows Instagram's interface.
- Clips have no audio and are trimmed to the food (about 6 seconds each).
- The logo is redrawn as a vector (SVG) from the Instagram profile image so it stays
  sharp at any size. Swap in the original logo file if the owner has one.
- The map shows an approximate area only, because no street number is published.
  "Load interactive map" embeds Google Maps once the page is hosted.
