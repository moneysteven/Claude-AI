1. What we collect and why

  • To deal with a booking: your name, phone number, email address, the service, date and time you asked for, the date and time you agreed to the Terms and Cancellation & Refund Policy, and how you paid. We use this to arrange your appointment. This is information we need to take steps you have asked for toward a booking, and then to carry it out. We use your email address to send your booking confirmation when Launthia confirms your appointment.
  • To reply to a bridal quote or a general message: whatever you choose to send us by text (SMS) or email.
  • If you join the Glow Club: your name, the way you asked us to contact you (email, SMS or WhatsApp) and that contact detail, your birthday (day and month only) if you choose to give it, and, if you tick the optional offers box, the date and time you agreed. We use your contact details to send what you agreed to: booking reminders and/or offers. We use your birthday only for a birthday greeting or birthday offer, and only if you agreed to receive offers.
  • We do not ask for your year of birth, payment-card details, or health information on this website. If you have an allergy or a skin condition, please tell Launthia by message before your appointment so she can take care. If you choose to tell us, that is health information and we will only use it to look after you in the session, and we will not post it.

You do not have to join the Glow Club to book an appointment. Marketing offers are optional.

2. How bookings and Glow Club joins reach Launthia

Booking and Glow Club details are stored securely with our database provider Supabase, so Launthia can manage your appointment. Only Launthia can sign in to see them. When you tap Pay & Book or Join, you can send your details to Launthia by text message (SMS) to 1-876-277-8913 or by email to Launthiawatson@yahoo.com, already filled in for you. She then keeps the conversation in SMS or email on her phone, like any other client message.

3. Who else may see information

  • WhatsApp is provided by Meta. If you chose WhatsApp for Glow Club messages, Meta and WhatsApp’s systems handle those messages. Their own privacy policy applies to that service.
  • If you send a text message, your phone carrier handles the SMS.
  • If you email us, your email provider and ours (Yahoo) handle the email.
  • Resend (email delivery) – used only to send Launthia a private email alert with your name, phone number and booking details when you book, and to send you your booking confirmation email when Launthia confirms your appointment.
  • Formspree (form email delivery) – used only to send Launthia a private email when you tell us you have sent a payment, with your name, phone number, booking details and the payment reference you typed (for a bank transfer, also your bank, branch, account type and only the last 4 digits of your account number). For a bank transfer we also ask for your own bank account details – bank, account name, account number, branch and account type – only so that Launthia can send your money back if she ever has to cancel; they are kept in her private booking system, which only she can see.
  • Bookings, Glow Club sign-ups and the owner sign-in use Supabase, our database provider. When you book, join the Glow Club or open the owner page, the site loads Supabase’s code library from cdn.jsdelivr.net, which may see your device’s IP address. When a page opens, the site also asks Supabase for the current prices and the days Launthia isn’t working; no personal details are sent, but Supabase may see your device’s IP address.
  • The site was built by Steven Scale Solutions (stevenscalesolutions.com). The site is hosted by Steven Scale Solutions using Hostinger. Like most websites, the host may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses and the time of a visit, and these are used only to keep the site running and secure.

  • We do not sell your information. We do not share it for other people’s marketing.

4. Information sent outside Jamaica

WhatsApp / Meta, Whop, Supabase, jsDelivr, Resend and Formspree (email delivery) are organisations that operate outside Jamaica, including in the United States. Jamaica’s Data Protection Act restricts sending personal data abroad unless there is a proper reason — for example you have consented, or it is necessary to take the steps you asked for toward your booking. By choosing to pay through Whop, or to send your booking or Glow Club message by SMS or email, you are asking us to use that service. You can instead email Launthiawatson@yahoo.com or call 1-876-277-8913.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it has no analytics. It uses your browser’s local storage only for things the site needs: remembering the times booked on this device, a short code (never your number) that stops the same number sending twice within 2 minutes, and, for Launthia only, keeping her signed in to the owner page. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing optional to turn on. If that ever changes, we will say so and ask before any optional tracker runs.

6. How long we keep information

Booking records are kept for 2 years for our records, then deleted. Glow Club details are kept until you unsubscribe. If you asked to join the Glow Club and then ask us to stop, we will take you off the list and only keep a minimal note that you asked us to stop, so we do not message you by mistake.
