Questions
Good to know


What is a pre-alert?
It's a quick notice telling us a package is coming: store, tracking number, contents and value. It lets us match and clear your package much faster.


When should I pre-alert?
As soon as you get your order confirmation or tracking number. Earlier is better, but you can also do it while the package is in transit.


Do I need to upload an invoice?
It's strongly recommended. Customs may need proof of value, and sending it early avoids delays.


Which stores can I shop from?
Any store that ships within the US: Amazon, Shein, eBay, Temu, Walmart and more.
