Questions
Good to know.

  

    
What is a pre-alert?
A short notice telling us a package is on its way: the store, tracking number, contents and value. It lets us match and clear your parcel much faster.

    
When should I pre-alert?
As soon as you receive your order confirmation or tracking number. You can also do it while the package is in transit.

    
Do I need to send an invoice?
We strongly recommend it. Customs may require proof of value, and sending it early prevents delays.

    
Which stores can I shop?
Any store that ships within the United States, including Amazon, Shein, eBay, Temu and Walmart.
