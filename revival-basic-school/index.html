Welcome from the Principal

Every child who comes through our gate will be greeted by name.

We are building a strong first step for the children of Revival, one that leads straight into Revival Primary School and beyond. We can't wait to meet your family.

Revival Basic to Revival Primary

One path from first day to Grade One

Our classes are planned together with Revival Primary School, so moving up to Grade One feels like the next step on a road your child already knows.

  1. 3

    Age 3 class

    Settling in

    Daily routines, new friends, talking and listening, first songs and stories.

  2. 4

    Age 4 class

    Growing

    Letters and sounds, counting and shapes, drawing and early writing.

  3. 5

    Age 5 class

    Getting ready

    Reading first words, writing their name, working with numbers and Grade One readiness.

  4. Grade One

    Revival Primary School

    A planned hand-over. Teachers meet, learning records move with your child, and your family already knows the school.

  • Visits each term

    Grade One teachers visit our Age 5 class, and our children tour Revival Primary.

  • Shared records

    With parents' consent, each child's learning profile goes ahead to their Grade One teacher.

  • Joint events

    Sports day, reading week and graduation, celebrated together.

  • Family support

    One familiar contact helps your family through the move up to primary school.

These areas can be matched to the early childhood curriculum and Early Childhood Commission standards the school will follow.

What children learn

Learning through play, every day

Young children learn best by doing. Each day mixes guided lessons with hands-on play, music and time outdoors.

Language and literacy

Rhymes, phonics and story time every day, building toward reading first words and writing names.

  • Phonics
  • Story time
  • Name writing

Numbers and shapes

Counting, sorting, patterns and measuring with real things: bottle caps, seeds and building blocks.

  • Counting
  • Patterns
  • Shapes

Music, art and drama

Painting, dancing, dress-up and pretend play, plus Jamaican folk songs and ring games.

  • Folk songs
  • Ring games
  • Painting

Active bodies

Running, jumping, ball games and balance outdoors, with threading, cutting and drawing for small hands.

  • Outdoor play
  • Ball skills
  • Fine motor

Science and our world

Plants, animals, weather and the people who make Revival work, explored in our school garden.

  • Garden
  • Weather
  • Community

Values and wellbeing

Morning devotion, good manners, sharing and taking turns, and learning to name big feelings.

  • Devotion
  • Kindness
  • Feelings

Our day

A day at Revival Basic

A calm, predictable rhythm helps little ones feel safe. Here is how a school day flows.

Live · Jamaica time

School days start at 7:30 AM with free play.

School hours

Days
Monday to Friday

7:30 – 8:30 AM

Pack every day

  • A water bottle with your child's name on it
  • A healthy snack and lunch
  • A spare change of clothes
  1. 7:30AM

    Arrival and free play

    Children are welcomed at the gate by name and choose a play corner.

  2. 8:30AM

    Devotion and circle time

    Songs, a short prayer, the weather chart and today's news.

  3. 9:00AM

    Language and literacy

    Phonics, letter games and a shared big-book story.

  4. 10:00AM

    Snack and outdoor play

    Fresh air, running games and the climbing frame.

  5. 10:45AM

    Numbers and shapes

    Counting songs, sorting and pattern work with real objects.

  6. 11:30AM

    Music, art and drama

    Painting, ring games, folk songs and pretend play.

  7. 12:00PM

    Lunch

    Washing hands, saying grace and eating together.

  8. 12:30PM

    Rest time

    Quiet time on mats with soft music.

  9. 1:15PM

    Story and home-time songs

    One last story, then we tidy up and get our bags.

  10. 1:30PM

    Home time

    Parents and guardians collect children at the gate.

Questions

What parents ask us

Can't find your answer? Call or message the office and we'll help.

Which ages does Revival Basic School take?

Children aged 3 to 5, in three classes: Age 3, Age 4 and Age 5. Your child's class is set by their age on 31 August of the school year.

What are the school hours?

Drop-off starts at 7:30 AM and classes begin at 8:30 AM. Pick-up is from 1:30 to 2:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Will my child move on to Revival Primary School?

Revival Basic is planned with Revival Primary so the move to Grade One is smooth. Grade One places follow the Ministry of Education's process, and we guide every family through it.

Are there school fees?

Fees will be shared at the parents' information evening before registration opens. Talk to the office if cost is a worry for your family.

Do children wear a uniform?

Yes. Uniform details and where to buy them will be posted on the notice board before opening day.

Is lunch provided?

Families send a snack and lunch each day. Clean drinking water is always available at school.
