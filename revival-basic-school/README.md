# Revival Basic School: website concept

A one-page demo website for Revival Basic School, the new basic school being
built in Revival that feeds into Revival Primary School. It is a concept to
show the Principal. Names, dates, contact details and figures are samples.

Open `index.html` in any browser to view it. No install or server needed.

## What's on the page

- **Logo and hero**: a starter logo (schoolhouse of toy blocks with a rising
  sun) and an animated illustration of blocks spelling REVIVAL and BASIC,
  with a crane lowering the last block
- **Build tracker**: progress meter, milestones and a live countdown to the
  target opening day
- **Principal's welcome**: space for a photo and message
- **Pathway**: Age 3, Age 4 and Age 5 classes leading into Grade One at
  Revival Primary, plus how the two schools work together
- **What children learn**: six learning areas
- **Our day**: daily schedule with a live "right now" card on Jamaica time
- **News and events**: filterable notice board and term dates
- **Help us build**: wish list with progress bars and ways to give
- **Register interest**: form with a class finder that works out the child's
  class from their date of birth (demo only, nothing is sent)
- **FAQ, contact details, light and dark themes, mobile menu**
- **Notes for the Principal**: a button in the top banner shows sticky notes
  that explain what to put in each section

## Before going live

1. Replace the sample phone, email, address, dates and wish list figures.
2. Add the Principal's message and photo, and real photos of the build.
3. Confirm the partnership details with Revival Primary School.
4. Connect the registration form to the school's email or WhatsApp.
5. Remove the "Website concept" banner and the Principal's notes.

To put it online for free, upload this folder to GitHub Pages, Netlify or
Cloudflare Pages.
