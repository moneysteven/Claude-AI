About

Detailing for paint, glass, trim and interiors

Rain & Shine Detailing is an auto detailing business in Jamaica.

Detailing goes further than a wash. It means cleaning, correcting and protecting a vehicle’s paint, glass, trim and interior, one panel at a time.
