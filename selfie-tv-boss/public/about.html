The story

Born for the mic.

Selfie TV Boss is a host, DJ, entertainer and actor who treats every event like live television. Whether it's a packed beach party in Montego Bay or an intimate launch in Kingston, he keeps the crowd locked in, the program on time and the cameras rolling.

“Every night is a broadcast. Every crowd deserves prime time.”

Behind the mic, Selfie TV Boss Entertainment handles the planning and promotion too. Promoters get one team that can build the hype before the night, run the show on the night, and put it on screen after.
